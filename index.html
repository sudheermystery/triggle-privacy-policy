Triggle Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 27, 2026

Aara Studios ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Triggle mobile game ("Triggle" or the "App").

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when you use Triggle.

1. Information and Data Used by the App

Triggle does not require you to provide information such as your name, postal address, or telephone number in order to play the game.

The App may process technical information associated with your device and your use of the App, including information that may be provided by third-party services used by the App.

2. Advertising

Triggle uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google AdMob and its advertising partners may process information such as device information, advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location, and information about interactions with advertisements, subject to applicable laws and your consent choices.

Advertising and consent settings may vary depending on your location and applicable privacy requirements.

3. Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required by applicable privacy laws, Triggle may ask you for consent before certain advertising or related data processing takes place.

Users may be provided with options to consent, decline consent, or manage their privacy choices. Your choices may affect the types of advertisements that can be shown.

4. Third-Party Services

Triggle may use third-party services that process information in connection with providing advertising, analytics, security, or other app functionality.

These third-party providers process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable requirements.

5. Children's Privacy

Triggle is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly request personal information from children under 13.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review the situation.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed in connection with the App. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
